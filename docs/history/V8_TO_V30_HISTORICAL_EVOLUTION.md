# Historical Architecture Evolution (V8 through V30)

This document archives historical release notes, hardening audits, and architectural evolution from V8 through V30 prior to the canonical V31 production architecture.

## Overview
- **V8-V25**: Exploratory research layers, single-symbol backtesting, secondary provider integrations, and initial point-in-time ingestion prototypes.
- **V26-V29**: India-wide accuracy hardening, two-tier row accounting, provenance tracking, and secondary provider decoupling.
- **V30**: Temporal and publication hardening, separation of calibration and conformal boundaries, and frozen production publication thresholds.

For current V31 operational guidelines, refer to the root [README.md](../README.md).

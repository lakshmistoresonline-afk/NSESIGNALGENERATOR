# Research methodology

## What was incorporated

### Intraday Trading AI India
- timestamp-based purged/out-of-sample validation principles
- baseline-first evaluation
- calibration and uncertainty reporting
- single-writer/data-contract mindset

### NSE Factor Backtest
- cross-sectional factors
- survivorship/look-ahead awareness
- realistic costs
- multiple-testing/Deflated-Sharpe awareness
- held-out evaluation

### BharatMarketAI
- walk-forward predictions
- next-period entry convention
- separation of research engine and UI
- explainable signal table

### EdgeLab
- pessimistic cost/slippage treatment
- signal-at-close/fill-next-bar convention
- strategy falsification mindset
- paper-signal workflow rather than live execution

## What is intentionally NOT claimed

The project does not claim a specific accuracy, Sharpe ratio, profitability, or future performance. Those numbers must be earned from the user's actual historical and forward data.
